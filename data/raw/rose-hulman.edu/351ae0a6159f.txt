Syllabus
Spring 2026
This is a research course. The majority of the course work will be
concerned with reading, presenting, and discussing the latest research related to LLMs and AGI,
as well as
Philosophy of Mind and Psychology, as they pertain to accomplishing AGI. As part of
the research, students are asked to use AI to quickly get to the forefront of the
field, to locate in-depth research papers, to ideate solutions, and the critically
examine solution approaches.
There will be two course projects:one to develop a small sandbox LLM that then serves
to extend it towards a system that exhibits AGI.
Prerequisites
CSSE 313 or permission of instructor.
Instructor
Michael Wollowski
Course Objectives
There are three main objectives for the course. They are:
To to understand requirements and paths forward towards
Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).
To advance the state of the art towards AGI.
To introduce students to academic research.

Citizenship
The success of this course depends on your active participation in
class.
Please come to class prepared and do things that contribute to your
learning and that of others.
Please avoid behavior that is distracting to your learning and that
of others.  Examples of unaccaptable behavior are: reading newspapers
in class, using your computer for things unrelated to the course,
sleeping in class. If I see such behavior I will ask you to explain
yourself. If you do not have a reasonable explanation, I will ask you
to drop the course and may give you a 0 for your participation score.
Academic Integrity
All the work you turn in has to be your own or has to be developed with
fellow students or in cooperation with AI. If you co-worker with fellow
students or AI, please give a brief indication of the nature of your contribution.
